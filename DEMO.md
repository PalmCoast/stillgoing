# Still Going — 45–60s demo

Record a phone-shaped window, or a phone itself. Leave the sound on. The beeps are part of the joke. Open rehearsal so the raid fits the cut:

`http://localhost:5173/?rehearsal=1`

Use whatever port `npm run dev` prints. Select **Laundry** and **5 min**. A 5 minute rehearsal lasts about 24 seconds and does not change the saved streak.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | Do this | Say this |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | Home, tip visible | Hold on the wordmark and the sticker | "Still Going. The game you play while you finish the chore." |
| 0:06–0:12 | Chore and duration | Tap Laundry, then 5 min. The dock names Laundry Drake. | "You start the raid when you start the chore." |
| 0:12–0:18 | Raid opens | Tap Start raid. Show the HP bar and the countdown. | "The clock is the boss. Laundry Drake only dies if you stay." |
| 0:18–0:28 | Still going? | A check pops up. Tap, hold, or swipe it. Combo ticks up. The boss flinches. | "Every minute or so it asks if you're still going. Answer, or the raid wipes." |
| 0:28–0:40 | Boss down | Let the short clock finish. Confetti, XP, streak. | "Last the timer and the boss goes down, because the laundry did." |
| 0:40–0:52 | Clear card | Hold on the card. Tap Save card so the PNG download flashes. | "The card is yours. The streak stays on the phone. No account." |
| 0:52–0:58 | Home, optional | Back home. If you already have a real clear, the streak is still there. | "Miss two checks, or walk off with the phone, and you just try again." |

Total is about 55 seconds if the check is fast and you don't linger on the menus. Cut any dead air between the check and the clear.

## Optional wipe insert (10–15s)

New take, same rehearsal URL, 5 minutes. Start the raid and do not touch the checks. Around a quarter of a minute in, the screen says **Wiped** and "Two checks slipped past." Tap **Try again**. No guilt copy, just another run.

To show the "left the screen" wipe instead, start the raid and switch apps for about four seconds.

## What not to spend time on

- Don't wait out a real 10 or 25 minute raid in this cut. Rehearsal is the demo clock. A real clear is what writes the streak.
- Don't open settings, the console, or the service worker.
- If the room is loud, tap the speaker once. The mute state sticks.
